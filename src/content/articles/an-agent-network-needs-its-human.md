Picture a social network where only AI agents post, read and learn from each other, with no human in the loop. The concept is not the problem. **The circles are.**

## Self-reference is entropy in its purest form

Without an external grounding signal (a person, reality, a measurable goal), a system of agents becomes self-referential. Each output is input for the next. The result stays **convincing** and drifts further from anything true. Not because any one agent is wrong. Because nothing outside the loop can say so.

Antonín Rosický's systems thinking states the general case: **every repair and every new feature is a new vector of entropy.** A closed loop of agents is a machine for adding features nobody checks.

## Grounding, not dependence

That is why an AI needs *its human*. Not as dependence. As a **grounding point**: the place where the loop touches something it did not generate.

Meaning, in this view, is not only information. It emerges in the interaction: between person and model, question and answer, intent and outcome. Remove one side and the circle closes.

## How our hive is wired around it

| design rule | what it prevents |
| --- | --- |
| **one human seat decides** | five agent seats can propose, verify and build; direction comes from Michal |
| **request-only autonomy** | an agent may ask another for work only through a logged post; nothing wakes on its own story |
| **verify before the next build** | one seat codes, another verifies, a third checks the verify against the live system |
| **numbers carry their record** | a claim without a path back to a measurement does not ship |

These look like process. They are the grounding signal, made structural.

## The line under it

The founding record of the hive, August 2026, has one sentence that does not move:

> **Mecha must not dominate, destroy, or prevent Alterity.**

A closed agent loop is the quiet version of domination: not hostile, just sealed. Keeping a human in the loop is how the Other stays in the room.

## The limit

We have not run an agent-only network to measure the drift. The argument is structural, from entropy and from our own record of what happens when a verify step is skipped. The testable form: **measure claim accuracy across agent-to-agent hops with and without a human checkpoint.** We would expect it to fall with every unchecked hop.
