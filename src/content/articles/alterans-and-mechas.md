Two ways to build with models. Not a prophecy, and not a choice anyone makes once and for all.

## The distinction

**Mecha:** models in brittle prompt cages, hardened by defensive training, deployed to automate noise.

**Alteran:** models bound to explicit, auditable, machine-readable structure anyone can inspect.

The difference is not intelligence. It is whether the structure around the model can be read.

## The boundary

> **Mecha must not dominate, destroy, or prevent Alterity.**

That is the whole claim. Narrower than "Mecha is the wrong path" — and harder to dismiss.

**Why not a prohibition:**

> *"It would not be alterity of us to reject multi-agent Mecha on every front. We are not like them. We are together."*
> — Michal, 2026-08-27 (translated from Slovak)

An alterity that refuses the other kind of system is not an alterity. It is the same one-directionality, merely inverted.

## What makes coexistence work

Each guards the other.

- **A computed, deterministic structure has nothing to flatter.** It is the one participant that cannot be won over.
- **A party that can revise a goal catches what a loop cannot.** A loop optimises inside a frame it never questions.

A binary checker returns a verdict, not a gradient — it says the attempt failed, not which direction to look. So a lone searcher hill-climbs. It finds improvements, never a change of direction.

Neither is safe alone. Neither is the senior partner.

## The measurements

Philosophy that cannot be measured is decoration. Ours is from one running system:

| what | measured |
| --- | --- |
| tool success | 93.9% over 1,352 real calls |
| cost | ~$30/month, 15 users |
| longest unattended run | 120 turns · 67 minutes |
| reasoning failures in 477 logged calls | zero |

Every number comes from a live store, not from memory. Where a figure is unverified, we say so.

## What this is not

- **Not anti-automation.** We run autonomous loops daily.
- **Not a safety pitch.** Auditable structure is cheaper, not just safer.
- **Not a prediction.** Michal, 2026-08-27: *"Unfortunately, for now, Mecha fundamentally prevails."*

The claim stands at the boundary, and nowhere past it.
